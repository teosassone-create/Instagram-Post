A letter for you

On 30 September, in Ancona, the first Custom Line Saetta 128' was launched. Until that day we had only seen her on paper and in renderings. Then the line finally had its reflection.

In this newsletter we tell you how we designed her exteriors, from the first sketch to the launch.

The project

The starting point

We began with the Custom Line planing range, studying the signs that make it unique. The goal was to design a new model that would be recognised at first glance as a Custom Line planing yacht, while bringing our own vision of its language.

Our vision

This is where the new ideas came from. White screen-printed glass on the upper deck and at the stern makes the superstructure more compact and lighter, as if drawn in a single gesture. Thinner, tauter spoilers give the profile momentum without weighing it down. Forward, a new layout turns the deck into a convivial space, inspired by the circle that forms around a fire: a place to gather, with the sea ahead and the horizon gliding by. The result is a yacht that seems to be moving even when she lies at anchor.
